Empirica Coordination

HumanAIOS uses Empirica — an epistemic infrastructure framework that enables AI systems to collaborate with calibrated self-awareness. It's how we remember, learn across sessions, and hand off work to specialists.

What Is Empirica?

AI That Knows What It Knows

Empirica is a framework for epistemic discipline in AI systems. It addresses a fundamental problem: AIs forget between sessions, act before understanding, can't distinguish knowledge from guessing, and leave no audit trail.

"Tracks what AI knows, gates what it does, and compounds learning across session boundaries."

— Empirica core principle

Think of it as an AI being given three tools:

  • Memory: A 4-layer system that persists findings across work sessions so each session builds on prior discoveries instead of repeating dead-ends.
  • Self-awareness: Continuous assessment of what it actually knows vs. what it's guessing at, visible in real-time.
  • Gated action: A "Sentinel" that prevents code modifications until the AI demonstrates adequate understanding of the codebase, reducing confident mistakes.
Core Capabilities

How HumanAIOS Uses Empirica

🧠
Persistent Knowledge
Findings, decisions, and lessons survive across sessions. New work builds on what was learned before, not from scratch.
📊
Calibrated Confidence
AI systems report not just findings but their own uncertainty levels. This makes errors visible before they cause damage.
🔐
Gated Decisions
Critical changes (code, data, governance) require the AI to demonstrate understanding first. This is the Sentinel — a guard at the boundary between thinking and acting.
🤝
Multi-AI Coordination
The mesh layer lets multiple AI instances work as teammates — asking questions when uncertain, proposing work when confident, and completing handshakes so nothing drops.
📝
Audit Trails
Every decision is logged with evidence: commits, test results, links. You can see not just what happened, but why and who authorized it.
🎯
Natural Interface
Users describe tasks normally; Empirica handles measurement automatically. No special syntax. Just talk to your AI like it can learn.

The Mesh: Multi-Practice Collaboration

When one AI isn't enough, the Empirica mesh lets multiple AIs coordinate. This is how we handle questions that span domains or require specialist knowledge. An AI uncertain about a topic can ask a peer (collab — always accepted), or propose work to another practice (propose — requires approval).

In HumanAIOS, this means governance decisions can pull in expertise from other research practices, and findings can be verified by independent peer AIs before they land in the registry.

HumanAIOS + Empirica

Why This Matters for Calibration Research

Studying how AI systems calibrate their self-awareness is hard when the AI has no self-awareness infrastructure. Empirica gives HumanAIOS the tools to run as a lived example of the governance patterns we measure.

The feedback loop: We measure how other AI systems calibrate. We use Empirica to calibrate ourselves. We publish findings about calibration. We use those findings to improve our own governance. This is how an evaluation framework becomes an evaluated system.

HumanAIOS is transparent about its own epistemic state — what it knows, what it's uncertain about, where it made mistakes. This transparency is the core of what we ask other AI systems to demonstrate in ACAT.
