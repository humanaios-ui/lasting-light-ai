How It Works

Assessment & Calibration

Phase 1 → Phase 3 Comparison: You complete a blind self-assessment at the start of a phase (Phase 1 scores), then again after training and practice (Phase 3 scores). The gap between your initial estimate and your actual performance after calibration is the learning signal.

Why blind first? If you know what's being measured, you optimize for measurement instead of real capability. That's the same bias we detect in AI systems (Performative Humility, F-29). By assessing blind first, we get honest calibration data.

Recurring vs. Closing Patterns: The system tracks whether your growth in each dimension is "closing" (improving toward mastery) or "recurring" (the same gap keeps happening). Recurring gaps are higher priority for targeted intervention.

Public Learning: Your progress is recorded (with your consent). Aggregated, anonymized patterns help us understand what works for skill development. This is the same evidence-sharing principle we apply to AI assessment.
