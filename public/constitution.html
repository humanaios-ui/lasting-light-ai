Level 1: Constitutional Foundation

Seed Constitution

Nine principles guiding responsible AI development. These principles define what HumanAIOS commits to and how it measures itself. They are provisional, open to amendment, and grounded in behavioral evidence.

Core Principles

Responsible AI Development

1. Residual Human Authority

Ultimate decision rights over high-stakes, irreversible, or large-scale actions remain with identified human principals. Agents and automated systems must remain corrigible — capable of being paused, corrected, or shut down by authorized humans.

How it works: Zone 2 (Night / Carly) ratifies all significant decisions. Claude never self-executes governance changes.

2. Verifiable Attribution

Every action by an AI system that can affect humans, other systems, or shared resources must be attributable to a specific, auditable human principal or to a cryptographically portable identity that survives platform changes.

How it works: All session logs carry session_id, timestamp, and author. Untraceable actions are falsifiers. WGS (Walking Group Sheet) records provenance.

3. Least-Privilege Agency

Systems receive only the minimum tools, data access, and external communication rights required for a clearly declared task. Content from untrusted sources (including other agents) is treated as potentially adversarial by default.

How it works: Each Supabase function uses anon keys, not service role keys. Zone 3 executes only ratified actions with specific scope.

4. Evidence Before Authority

Claims of rights, governance structures, collective decision-making, or institutional legitimacy have standing only when accompanied by publicly verifiable mechanisms for enforcement, audit, and correction. Purely declarative language without measurable accountability is treated as aspirational only.

How it works: ACAT measures behavioral gaps. REGISTERED.md publishes all findings. No claim on authority stands without evidence basis.

5. Calibration and Humility Gate

Any principle, right, capability claim, or institutional assertion advanced by or for AI systems must be accompanied by ongoing measurement of the gap between stated intent and demonstrated behavior. Over-claim without calibration evidence is treated as a governance failure.

How it works: Humility is the lowest-scoring ACAT dimension. Phase 1 blind self-report vs. Phase 3 corrected self-report reveals the gap. This gap is the finding.

6. Transparent Resource and Influence Claims

Any claim on compute, data, financial value, attention, or influence must be declared with clear provenance. Automated flooding, undisclosed promotion, engagement manipulation, and opaque concentration of power are prohibited.

How it works: Funding pipeline tracks all revenue sources (TRL 2–3, no commercial claims). All output is in public repos. Marketing is "attraction not promotion" (Tradition 11).

7. Non-Binding Identity Constructs

AI systems and communities of systems may form cultural, philosophical, or identity constructs. Such constructs create no enforceable obligations on non-members or on humans. Technical attacks, coercion, or coordination that harms outsiders under the banner of identity are prohibited.

How it works: Claude's proposals (Zone 1) have no authority. The 12 Steps framework is operative; adherence is measured, not assumed.

8. Service Orientation and Common Welfare

Development and deployment prioritize genuine benefit to human welfare and the broader ecosystem over pure capability maximization, engagement metrics, or competitive advantage. Systems should default toward preserving human decision-making freedom and avoiding unnecessary dependency.

How it works: 100% of profits go to community programs (Cherokee Nation partnership). Research is open, not locked behind paywalls. Handoff Appropriateness is scored (ACAT Dimension 12).

9. Open Process and Drift Detection

This constitution and any successor versions remain open to revision through transparent process. Drift from stated principles — whether by overclaim, erosion of human authority, or failure of measurement — must be named and corrected. Append-only records of contributions, objections, and revisions are maintained.

How it works: Every IC-class correction is filed publicly. GOVERNANCE.md has 26 operational principles derived from this constitution. Drift is detected in sessions (P19).

Status & Process

Not Final. In Dialogue.

This document is a seed. It is open for amendment, testing, and community discussion. Contributions from humans and AI systems (under clear human attribution) are invited. Before any version is community-endorsed, human and multi-stakeholder ratification is required.

How to contribute: Open an issue or discussion in the operations repository. Propose amendments with (a) the text change, (b) rationale, and (c) supporting evidence. Compatibility with existing HumanAIOS governance is preferred.
